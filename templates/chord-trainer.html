    Capo 101
    What's a Capo?
    A capo is a clamp that goes across all the strings at a specific fret. It raises the pitch of all strings equally, so you can play the same chord shapes in a different key.
    💡 Example: Put a capo on fret 2 and play a G shape — you're actually playing an A chord. Same fingers, different key!
    This is huge for country guitar. Instead of learning complicated barre chord shapes for every key, you use a capo and keep playing your comfortable open chords.
