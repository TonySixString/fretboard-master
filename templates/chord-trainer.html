    Capo 101
    What's a Capo?
    A capo is a clamp that goes across all the strings at a specific fret. It raises the pitch of all strings equally, so you can play the same chord shapes in a different key.
    We use a capo because chords with open strings sound different than barre chords where our fingers are touching every string. If you need to use a capo for any of our lessons, it will tell you which fret to put the capo on at the top of the chord chart.
